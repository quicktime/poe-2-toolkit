# 🗺️ Path of Exile 2 Waystone Optimization Guide (v0.3)

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: This is for Path of Exile 2, NOT Path of Exile 1

Path of Exile 2 uses a completely different endgame system than PoE 1. Waystones are consumable items that grant access to endgame maps through the Atlas of Worlds.

---

## 📊 PoE 2 Waystone System Overview

### Key Differences from PoE 1:
- **One Life Per Map**: Death loses the waystone and all mechanics
- **Generic Waystones**: Insert into Atlas to generate zones
- **Tower Mechanics**: Precursor Towers allow tablet placement for area bonuses
- **Tier System**: Tiers 1-16 (T15+ is true endgame farming)
- **No Map Bosses by Default**: Must kill all rare monsters to complete

## 🔧 EXACT CRAFTING STEPS FOR POE 2

### Step 1: Acquire Base Waystones
**Methods:**
- Drop from monsters (starts in Cruel Act 3)
- Purchase from Doryani (5,000+ gold)
- Vendor Recipe: 3 same-tier waystones → 1 higher tier

**Target:** Tier appropriate for your level
- Level 60-70: T1-5
- Level 70-80: T5-10
- Level 80+: T10-15
- Endgame farming: T15-16

### Step 2: Upgrade Waystone Rarity
**CRITICAL:** Never run white (normal) waystones!

**Currency Usage:**
1. **Orb of Transmutation**: Normal → Magic (1-2 mods)
2. **Regal Orb**: Magic → Rare (adds 1 mod)
3. **Orb of Alchemy**: Normal → Rare (4-6 mods)

**Cost:** 1-3 chaos equivalent

### Step 3: Target Key Modifiers
**For Experience:**
- "Dense" - Increased Monster Pack Size
- "Dangerous" - Additional Rare Monsters
- "Bountiful" - Increased Waystone Drops (sustain)

**Avoid:**
- Reduced recovery
- Reflect damage
- Extreme monster damage (unless overgeared)

### Step 4: Utilize Atlas Passive Tree
**Essential Early Nodes (by T4 completion):**
1. **Constant Crossroads** - Direct waystone drop buff
2. **Fortunate Path** - Save currency on upgrades
3. **The High Road** - 20% chance for higher tier drops

### Step 5: Tower & Tablet Strategy
**When you find a Precursor Tower:**
1. Use a waystone with 6 modifiers (allows 3 tablets)
2. Place tablets immediately after clearing tower
3. Stack effects: quantity and strongbox chance stack!
4. Farm all maps in irradiated radius

## 💰 Investment & Returns

### Cost per Waystone:
- **Basic (Magic):** 1-2 chaos
- **Good (Rare with 4+ mods):** 3-5 chaos
- **Optimal (6 mods for towers):** 5-10 chaos

### Expected Returns:
- **Waystone Sustain:** 1.2-1.5x return with proper Atlas
- **Experience Rate:** 30-50% faster than white waystones
- **T15+ Surplus:** Eventually self-sustaining

## 🏃 Execution Strategy

### Mapping Flow:
1. **Pre-roll waystones** in batches of 10-20
2. **Prioritize tower maps** when found
3. **Clear all rare monsters** (required for completion)
4. **Don't die** - you lose everything!
5. **Buy from Doryani** if you run out

### Atlas Progression:
1. **T1-10:** Focus on sustain and Atlas completion
2. **T10-14:** Start targeting specific farming regions
3. **T15+:** True endgame - sustain becomes easy

## 📊 Optimization Tips

### For Experience:
- Focus on **monster density** over item quantity
- **Chain similar tilesets** for muscle memory
- **Skip optional content** unless high density
- **Group play** still provides XP bonus

### For Sustain:
- **Always upgrade waystones** to at least Magic
- **Use Fortunate Path** Atlas passive ASAP
- **Complete Atlas bonus objectives**
- **Tablet usage** dramatically improves drops

### Currency Saving:
- **Bulk buy** from other players
- **Use vendor recipe** for tier upgrades
- **Save Regals** - use Alchemy instead
- **Don't over-invest** in low-tier waystones
