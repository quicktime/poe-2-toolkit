# 🎯 ACCURATE PATH OF EXILE 2 WAYSTONE OPTIMIZATION STRATEGY

## ⚠️ Critical PoE 2 Currency Differences from PoE 1:
- **Exalted Orbs** are the base currency (not Chaos)
- **No Chaos Orb spam** - Chaos Orbs work differently in PoE 2
- **Currency is much more valuable** - Crafting is more deliberate

---

## 📊 PoE 2 Waystone Strategy Overview

- **Goal**: Maximum Experience & Atlas Progression
- **Expected Cost**: 0.5-2 Exalted Orbs per waystone
- **Experience Bonus**: +40-80% faster leveling
- **Clear Speed**: 2-4 minutes per map
- **Success Rate**: 85% sustain with proper Atlas

## 🎯 Target Waystone Modifiers for Experience

### Priority 1 (Essential):
1. **"Dense"** - Increased Monster Pack Size
2. **"Dangerous"** - Additional Rare Monsters
3. **"Bountiful"** - Increased Waystone Drop Chance

### Priority 2 (Valuable):
4. **"Valuable"** - Increased Rarity of Items
5. **Tower Presence** - Enables tablet placement
6. **"Irradiated"** - Precursor energy effects

### Avoid:
- **Reflect** - Can instantly kill
- **No Recovery** - Extremely dangerous
- **Temporal effects** - Slows clear speed

## 📝 EXACT CRAFTING STEPS FOR POE 2

### 🔷 STEP 1: Acquire Base Waystone
**Action**: Get appropriate tier waystone

**Methods**:
- **Drops**: From monsters (best from rare/magic packs)
- **Doryani**: Purchase with gold
  - T1-3: 5,000 gold
  - T4-6: 10,000 gold
  - T7-9: 20,000 gold
  - T10+: 40,000+ gold
- **Vendor**: 3 same-tier → 1 higher tier

**Target by Level**:
- Level 65-70: T1-4
- Level 70-75: T4-7
- Level 75-80: T7-10
- Level 80-85: T10-13
- Level 85+: T14-16

**Cost**: 0.1-0.3 Exalted Orbs (or gold equivalent)

---

### 🔷 STEP 2: Check for Precursor Tower
**Action**: Look for tower icon

**If Tower Present**:
- HIGH VALUE waystone
- Worth investing more currency
- Save for 6-modifier crafting

**If No Tower**:
- Standard crafting approach
- Focus on sustain modifiers

**Tower Chance**: ~10% of waystones

---

### 🔷 STEP 3: Upgrade to Magic Rarity
**Action**: Use Orb of Transmutation

**Currency**: Orb of Transmutation
**Cost**: ~0.01 Exalted Orbs
**Result**: 1-2 random modifiers

**Decision Tree**:
- ✅ Has "Dense" or "Dangerous" → Keep, proceed to Step 4
- ❌ Has bad mods → Scour and retry (0.05 Exalted)
- 🔄 Neutral → Proceed if currency limited

---

### 🔷 STEP 4: Add Second Modifier
**Action**: Use Augmentation if only 1 mod

**Currency**: Orb of Augmentation
**Cost**: ~0.02 Exalted Orbs
**When**: Only if 1 modifier on Magic item

**Skip if**: Already has 2 modifiers

---

### 🔷 STEP 5: Upgrade to Rare
**Action**: Make waystone Rare for more modifiers

**Option A - Regal Orb** (From Magic):
- **Cost**: ~0.2 Exalted Orbs
- **Result**: Adds 1 modifier, becomes Rare
- **Use when**: Good Magic base

**Option B - Orb of Alchemy** (From Normal):
- **Cost**: ~0.15 Exalted Orbs
- **Result**: 4-6 random modifiers
- **Use when**: Starting fresh

**Target**: 4+ beneficial modifiers

---

### 🔷 STEP 6: Optimize Modifiers (PoE 2 Method)
**Action**: Improve waystone WITHOUT Chaos spam

**Methods Available in PoE 2**:

**A) Orb of Annulment** (Remove bad mod):
- **Cost**: ~0.5 Exalted Orbs
- **Use**: Remove one bad modifier
- **Risk**: Might remove good mod

**B) Lesser Jeweller's Orb** (Reroll values):
- **Cost**: ~0.1 Exalted Orbs
- **Use**: Reroll numeric values
- **Safe**: Keeps modifiers, changes numbers

**C) Essence Crafting** (Guaranteed mod):
- **Cost**: 0.2-1 Exalted (depends on essence)
- **Use**: Force specific modifier
- **Best for**: Targeting pack size or rarity

**D) Accept and Run** (Most common):
- If 2+ good mods, just run it
- Don't over-invest in non-tower waystones

---

### 🔷 STEP 7: Tower Waystone Optimization
**Action**: Maximize modifiers for tablet slots

**For Tower Waystones Only**:
1. Get Rare with good base (Steps 1-5)
2. **Add modifiers** if <6:
   - Use Exalted Orb: 1 Exalted Orb
   - Adds 1 random modifier
3. Target 6 modifiers total
4. **Investment**: 1-3 Exalted Orbs

**Why**: 6 modifiers = 3 tablet slots = massive bonuses

---

### 🔷 STEP 8: Corruption (OPTIONAL)
**Action**: Vaal Orb for implicit

**Currency**: Vaal Orb
**Cost**: ~0.1 Exalted Orbs
**Risk**: Can brick waystone

**Only if**: You have duplicates
**Skip**: For most waystones
