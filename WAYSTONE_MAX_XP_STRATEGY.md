## 🎯 Target Modifiers (MUST HAVE)

1. **"Enlightening" suffix**: +30-50% increased Experience gain
2. **"Populous" prefix**: +20-40% increased Pack Size
3. **"Multitudinous" prefix**: 3-6 additional Packs of Monsters
4. **"Advanced" suffix**: +1-3 to Monster Level
5. **"Fleet" suffix** (optional): Monsters have increased Movement Speed (faster clear)

## ❌ Avoid These Modifiers

- **No Regeneration** - Extremely dangerous, will kill most builds
- **Reflected Damage** - Can kill you instantly
- **Twin Boss** - Slows down completion significantly
- **Reduced Recovery Rate** - Dangerous for most builds
- **Hexproof** - Problematic for curse-dependent builds

## 📝 EXACT CRAFTING STEPS

### Step 1: Start with Normal Waystone
- **Action**: Obtain a Tier 5+ waystone (level 75+ area)
- **Cost**: 0.5-5 chaos (base waystone cost)
- **Note**: Higher tiers give more base experience

### Step 2: Apply Quality (CRITICAL - Must be Normal rarity!)
- **Action**: Use 4 Cartographer's Chisels to reach 20% quality
- **Currency**: Cartographer's Chisel x4
- **Cost**: 1 chaos total
- **⚠️ WARNING**: MUST be done while waystone is Normal rarity!
- **Result**: +20% quantity (affects experience drops)

### Step 3: Transmute to Magic
- **Action**: Use Orb of Transmutation
- **Currency**: Orb of Transmutation
- **Cost**: 0.1 chaos
- **Result**: Waystone becomes Magic with 1-2 random modifiers

### Step 4: Check Modifiers
- **Good outcome**: If you get "Enlightening" or "Populous", continue to Step 5
- **Bad outcome**: Use Orb of Scouring and restart from Step 2
- **Cost if reroll**: 1 chaos (Scouring Orb)

### Step 5: Upgrade to Rare
- **Option A (if Magic)**: Use Regal Orb
  - Cost: 3 chaos
  - Result: Adds one additional modifier
- **Option B (if Normal)**: Use Orb of Alchemy
  - Cost: 2 chaos
  - Result: 4-6 random modifiers

### Step 6: Roll for Optimal Modifiers (Most Important Step)
- **Action**: Use Chaos Orbs until you get at least 2 target modifiers
- **Currency**: Chaos Orb
- **Cost**: 5-20 chaos (average 10)
- **Target Outcome**:
  ```
  Ideal waystone has:
  ✅ +40% increased Experience (Enlightening)
  ✅ +30% increased Pack Size (Populous)
  ✅ +5 additional Packs (Multitudinous)
  ✅ +2 Monster Level (Advanced)
  ❌ NO dangerous mods (reflect, no regen, etc.)
  ```

### Step 7: Divine for Perfect Rolls (OPTIONAL - High Budget)
- **Action**: Use Divine Orb if modifiers are good but rolls are low
- **Currency**: Divine Orb
- **Cost**: 200 chaos
- **Note**: Only worth it for perfect waystones you'll run many times
- **Skip**: Most players should skip this step

### Step 8: Add Exalted Modifier (OPTIONAL - Very High Budget)
- **Action**: If waystone has open prefix/suffix, add with Exalted Orb
- **Currency**: Exalted Orb
- **Cost**: 150 chaos
- **Risk**: Might add bad modifier
- **Skip**: Only for high-budget farming

### Step 9: Corrupt for Implicit (HIGH RISK - NOT RECOMMENDED)
- **Action**: Vaal Orb can add powerful implicit
- **Currency**: Vaal Orb
- **Cost**: 1 chaos
- **Possible Outcomes**:
  - 25% chance: Add beneficial implicit (+level, +experience)
  - 25% chance: Change to different waystone
  - 25% chance: Reroll all modifiers (brick)
  - 25% chance: No change (just corrupted)
- **⚠️ WARNING**: Only do this with duplicate perfect waystones!
