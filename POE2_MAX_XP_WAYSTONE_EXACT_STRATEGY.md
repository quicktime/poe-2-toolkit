## 📝 EXACT CRAFTING STEPS

### 🔷 STEP 1: Acquire Your Base Waystone
**Action**: Get appropriate tier waystone for your level

**Methods**:
- **Primary**: Kill monsters in previous maps (best drop rate from rare monsters)
- **Backup**: Purchase from Doryani for gold
  - T1-3: 5,000 gold
  - T4-6: 10,000 gold
  - T7-9: 20,000 gold
  - T10+: 40,000+ gold
- **Vendor Recipe**: 3 waystones of same tier = 1 waystone of next tier

**Target Tiers by Level**:
- Level 65-70: Use T1-4 waystones
- Level 70-75: Use T4-7 waystones
- Level 75-80: Use T7-10 waystones
- Level 80-85: Use T10-13 waystones
- Level 85+: Use T14-16 waystones

**Cost**: 0.5-2 chaos (or equivalent gold)

---

### 🔷 STEP 2: Check for Precursor Tower
**Action**: Look for tower icon on your waystone

**If Tower Present**:
- This waystone is now HIGH PRIORITY
- Save for 6-modifier crafting (see Step 7)
- Towers allow tablet placement for massive area bonuses

**If No Tower**:
- Continue with normal crafting
- Tower waystones are ~10% chance

**Cost**: No additional cost

---

### 🔷 STEP 3: Upgrade to Magic Rarity
**Action**: Use Orb of Transmutation on Normal waystone

**Currency**: Orb of Transmutation
**Cost**: 0.1 chaos
**Result**: Waystone becomes Magic with 1-2 random modifiers

**Check Results**:
- ✅ Good: Has "Dense" or "Dangerous" → Continue to Step 4
- ❌ Bad: Has negative mods → Use Orb of Scouring, restart at Step 3
- 🔄 Neutral: Acceptable mods → Continue to Step 4

**Success Rate**: ~30% for good modifier

---

### 🔷 STEP 4: Augment if Needed (Magic Only)
**Action**: Add second modifier if waystone only has one

**Currency**: Orb of Augmentation
**Cost**: 0.2 chaos
**When to Use**: Only if Magic waystone has 1 modifier

**Result**: Adds one additional random modifier

**Skip if**: Waystone already has 2 modifiers

---

### 🔷 STEP 5: Upgrade to Rare
**Action**: Transform waystone to Rare rarity

**Option A - From Magic (Recommended)**:
- **Currency**: Regal Orb
- **Cost**: 2-3 chaos
- **Result**: Adds 1 modifier, waystone becomes Rare
- **Use When**: You have good Magic base

**Option B - From Normal**:
- **Currency**: Orb of Alchemy
- **Cost**: 1.5-2 chaos
- **Result**: 4-6 random modifiers, waystone becomes Rare
- **Use When**: Starting fresh or low on Regals

**Target**: At least 4 total modifiers

---

### 🔷 STEP 6: Optimize with Chaos Orbs
**Action**: Reroll until optimal modifier combination

**Currency**: Chaos Orb
**Cost per Roll**: 1 chaos
**Average Rolls Needed**: 3-8 (depending on standards)

**Minimum Acceptable** (Budget):
```
✅ Dense (20%+ pack size)
✅ Any other beneficial mod
✅ No instant-death mods
Expected Rolls: 3-4 chaos
```

**Good Target** (Standard):
```
✅ Dense (25%+ pack size)
✅ Dangerous (5+ rare monsters)
✅ 1-2 other beneficial mods
✅ No dangerous mods
Expected Rolls: 5-7 chaos
```

**Perfect Roll** (High Investment):
```
✅ Dense (30%+ pack size)
✅ Dangerous (7+ rare monsters)
✅ Bountiful (25%+ waystone drops)
✅ Valuable (30%+ rarity)
✅ 2 other beneficial mods
✅ No negative mods
Expected Rolls: 10-20 chaos
```

**Pro Tip**: Save waystones with 2+ good mods even if not perfect

---

### 🔷 STEP 7: Maximize for Tower Waystones (SPECIAL)
**Action**: Get to 6 modifiers for maximum tablet slots

**Requirements**: Waystone must have Precursor Tower

**Process**:
1. Follow Steps 1-6 to get good Rare waystone
2. **Use Exalted Orb** if waystone has <6 mods
   - Cost: 100-150 chaos (only for towers!)
   - Result: Adds 1 modifier
3. Repeat until 6 modifiers total

**Why**: 6 modifiers = 3 tablet slots = MASSIVE area bonuses

---

### 🔷 STEP 8: Optional Corruption (EXTREME RISK)
**Action**: Corrupt for potential benefits

**Currency**: Vaal Orb
**Cost**: 1 chaos
**When**: ONLY with duplicate perfect waystones

**Possible Outcomes**:
- 25%: Beneficial implicit (higher tier, more monsters)
- 25%: Becomes different waystone
- 25%: Rerolls all modifiers
- 25%: No change (just corrupted)

**Recommendation**: SKIP unless you have 5+ identical good waystones
